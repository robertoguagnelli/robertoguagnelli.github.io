01 / MECHANISM DESIGN · 2025 TEAM PROJECT

Single-motor cutter for BIC extrusions

Designed and built a synchronized feed-and-cut prototype in response to inconsistent cut lengths in a BIC production process.

690 RPMreported operating speed
1 motorsynchronized feed and cutting
Mechanism, prototype and test videos +

The team replaced a continuous motor plus servo concept with one motor driving the feed rollers and cutting mechanism. A reduction gear train increased available torque; a Geneva mechanism translated continuous rotation into indexed motion. The design included a double-blade cutter and a revolution counter for monitoring.

We modeled the parts in SolidWorks, integrated the feeder, gearbox and cutter into one enclosure, then built the prototype using laser-cut panels, 3D-printed parts and machined components. The demonstration videos show the internal transmission running and the cutting process through the enclosure.

The team reported 690 RPM and stable synchronization, while identifying feed-wheel slip, friction losses, blade sharpness, controller failures and gear breakage under cutting loads. The slide's 2,760 pieces/min figure is a theoretical rate derived from mechanism speed, not a measured production result.

02 / FAILURE ANALYSIS · TEAM PROJECT

Why an extrusion screw fractured

A forensic investigation of an H13 steel screw after a fracture during PLA + ABS pelletizing.

H13nitrided tool steel
605 MPaestimated shear stress at 2× torque
Evidence and findings +

The team examined a 19 mm screw and barrel assembly (L/D 30) through visual inspection, Rockwell and Vickers hardness, metallography, SEM, EDS and a torsional stress estimate. Inspection showed grinding marks and local geometric discontinuities. Hardness and microstructure were consistent with heat-treated, nitrided H13 away from the damaged region.

SEM revealed a distinct interface, porosity and cracks; EDS found material with a different composition in the lighter region. The estimated 2× torque case reached 605 MPa, below the assumed 650 MPa shear limit for sound material. The team's probable explanation was localized torsional overload at an earlier, nonuniform repair, rather than failure of intact H13 alone.

The original fracture surface was damaged after the break, so the precise crack initiation and any fatigue contribution could not be confirmed.

03 / THERMAL DESIGN · TEAM PROJECT

Heat exchangers for atmospheric water

Conceptual evaporator and condenser sizing for a vapor-compression system targeting 1 kg of water per hour.

17.14 kWpeak modeled cooling load · Chihuahua
2.49 kWpeak modeled cooling load · Chetumal
Design and climate comparison +

The team modeled a stationary vapor-compression cycle using R-1234yf. A psychrometric balance estimated the cooling needed to condense water from ambient air, then informed compressor selection and finned evaporator and condenser concepts for Chihuahua and Chetumal.

Chihuahua · dry climateCondensation was modeled as seasonal, from June to October. The largest modeled evaporator and condenser tube totals were 8.68 m and 1.63 m.
Chetumal · humid climateConditions supported condensation throughout the modeled year. The corresponding tube totals were 0.70 m and 0.33 m.

This was a modeled design, not a measured prototype. The presentation lists a 17.14 kW peak cooling load for Chihuahua but a 12.54 kW selected compressor capacity; peak-condition sizing requires reconciliation before the 1 kg/h target can be claimed there.
