04 / FLIGHT DYNAMICS · PYTHON + XFLR5

From the state matrix to the aircraft response.

I evaluated longitudinal stability in the empty and loaded configurations using aerodynamic stability derivatives, a linear state-space model and its eigenvalues. The analysis identified the phugoid and short-period modes and characterized their damping and frequency.

Aerodynamics

Stability derivatives

XFLR5 supported the aerodynamic analysis. Stability derivatives, mass properties and the trim condition formed the inputs to the longitudinal model.

Python

Assemble A

The longitudinal state matrix describes how small disturbances in the flight state evolve and interact around a trimmed condition.

Modal analysis

Solve eigenvalues

The eigenvalues of A reveal the modal decay rates and oscillation frequencies, allowing the empty and loaded states to be compared.

A conventional body-axis state ordering is shown to explain the method. This symbolic structure does not reproduce the original numerical matrix. The coefficients combine aerodynamic, inertial and trim effects; the last row expresses δθ̇ = δq.

Reading the eigenvalues

Decay and oscillation in one result.

det(λI − A) = 0

λ = σ ± iωd

A negative real part, σ, indicates a decaying mode. The imaginary part, ωd, gives its damped angular frequency. The two complex-conjugate pairs are interpreted as the phugoid and short-period modes.

For asymptotic stability, every eigenvalue of the longitudinal model must have a negative real part.

Modal quantities

ωn = √(σ² + ωd²)

ζ = −σ / ωn

Natural frequency, ωn
Characterizes the mode's underlying time scale.

Damping ratio, ζ
Describes decay relative to that natural frequency.

Damped frequency and natural frequency are distinct. No aircraft-specific eigenvalues, derivatives or modal values are published here.

Aircraft-level implication

Check the dynamics throughout the payload envelope.

The report links the forward CG shift under loading to increased static margin and pitching stiffness, together with a change in short-period behavior. I used the empty-versus-loaded comparison to assess the longitudinal response across loading states, beyond a static CG check alone.

06 / PAYLOAD SYSTEM

Retain each bottle. Cover the complete payload.

Bottle-count scoring → Rigid supports → Retainer iteration → Fabric cover → Installed system

01 / Iterate the PETG retainer

First geometry: higher insertion effort and occasional fracture.

Revised geometry: changed outer radius and finger-access tabs improved handling.

Repeated insertion cyclesProlonged static-load check

Insertion force was not measured. These checks do not establish a long-term creep lifetime.

02 / Cover with fabric + Velcro

Rule: payload could not be directly exposed to airflow.

Response: cover the supported bottles with fabric secured by Velcro.

  • Negligible mass penalty
  • Rule compliance
  • Simple installation
  • Fits the existing support system

A lightweight, unconventional and rule-driven solution.
