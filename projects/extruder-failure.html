Extruder screw failure analysis

Reconstructing a fracture from physical evidence.

01 / FAILURE CONTEXT

What caused the screw to fail?

A 19 mm extrusion screw, L/D = 30, specified as quenched, ground and nitrided H13 tool steel. Its function: transport, compress, melt and push polymer through the extrusion system.

Service had changed from conventional polymers to reinforced bioplastics, introducing different friction and abrasive conditions. The final event occurred during PLA + ABS pelletizing, after approximately 500 g of processed material.

  1. Abnormal noise
  2. Polymer discoloration
  3. Loss of output; purge unsuccessful
  4. Disassembly revealed the fracture

05 / FAILURE RECONSTRUCTION

The combined evidence supports a probable cause.

  1. Local weakness

    Prior repair / nonuniform region / defects.

  2. Changed service

    Different polymers and changed friction / abrasive conditions.

  3. Final event

    Possible jam or increase in torque.

  4. Probable failure mode

    Localized torsional overload in a weakened, previously repaired region.

Evidence limitations

  • Fatigue could not be confirmed: the original fracture surface had been damaged.
  • Filler identification remained tentative: similarity to E2307-16 was discussed only as a possibility.
  • Repair records were unavailable: the original repair procedure could not be established.
  • The torsional model is simplified: it does not represent local defects or stress concentrations.
