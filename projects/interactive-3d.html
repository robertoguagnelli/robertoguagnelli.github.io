02 / BEHAVIOR & ANIMATION INTEGRATION

Alien Forest.

Razzleberries · Collaborative project
Jul–Nov 2022 · Minecraft Bedrock

Developed six custom creatures with distinct behaviors, animations and sounds: a passive light bug, a tameable companion fox, a rideable high-jumping stag, a flying mount and two hostile creatures.

The work brought models, textures, behavior definitions and animation controllers together into interactive entities.

TECHNICAL FOCUS / TERROR

A mount with linked flight states.

The player tames Terror with golden apples, equips a saddle through its inventory and controls its direction while riding. Holding the jump input makes it ascend; releasing the input produces a slow, gliding descent.

Animation controllers connect these interactions with wing deployment and flapping.

  1. Tame & equipGolden apples establish ownership; the inventory accepts a saddle.
  2. Take off & climbThe jump input initiates ascent, with wings unfolding and flapping.
  3. Release & glideThe mount descends slowly while the rider controls its direction.

CHALLENGE

Make animation follow the entity state.

Flight combined several interacting behaviors and animation controllers. Wing deployment needed to begin when Terror left the ground, rather than remain disconnected from its movement.

APPROACH & RESULT

Use the ground state as the trigger.

I researched existing creature implementations and community code discussions, then added a tag representing whether Terror was on the ground. When that state became false, it triggered the airborne animation.

This connected the animation transition to the creature’s actual ground state.
