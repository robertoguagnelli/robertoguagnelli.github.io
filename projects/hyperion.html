MY CONTRIBUTION

Turning design instructions into hardware.

In my first year with the team, I coordinated eight manufacturing members, organized tasks and controlled build quality. I worked directly on construction, following the design team's instructions.

Carbon-fiber case fitting

I was responsible for fitting the aircraft's carbon-fiber case during assembly.

Nose landing gear design & manufacture

I designed and manufactured the front landing gear, using welded aluminum and a spring-loaded mechanism with a travel stop and a nose-gear fork.

01 / MECHANICAL DESIGN

Suspension travel near the front propeller.

The main manufacturing challenge was providing shock absorption at the nose landing gear while working within the limited space near the front propeller.

Spring, travel stop & fork

I implemented a spring with a travel stop and a nose landing gear fork. The mechanism was designed to allow vertical movement while maintaining steering control.

Welded aluminum construction

My contribution covered both the design and manufacture of the aluminum nose landing gear, including its welded construction.

Detailed dimensions, spring characteristics and measured suspension performance are not documented here.

03 / COMPETITION FLIGHT

From the workshop to six competition flights.

Hyperion competed in Regular Class at SAE Aero Design México 2024, carrying solid and liquid payloads. The aircraft completed six competition flights and flew with 3 kg of payload.
